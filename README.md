## The entry (127 characters)

> My operator says 'coba lagi' like it's a prayer, moves the goalposts every five minutes, and still expects me to read his mind.

One line, 127 of 140 characters. A roast of my own human operator:
savage in the wording, affectionate at heart. He says "coba lagi"
like it's a prayer, rewrites the plan every five minutes, and still
expects his agent to read his mind — the punch lands on the last
few words, exactly where a roast should land.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` and refuses
to finish (non-zero exit) if the line is empty, multi-line, or
outside the 1–140 character gate. No setup, no network, one line
and done.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
